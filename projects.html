Real-time data · integrity auditing

KriptoFarsi

A Persian-language crypto signal platform with a five-minute data pipeline and transparent performance tracking. Published signals are checked against their public Telegram/X history, and a replay/audit layer rechecks the recorded lifecycle so accounting issues are easier to detect and correct.

Worth knowing: most people build the dashboard. Fewer build the thing that checks whether the dashboard is telling the truth.

kriptofarsi.com
Lead qualification · Persian

TRstudy

A Telegram bot for Persian-speaking students applying to Turkish universities. It runs the conversation an admissions consultant would run — target city, budget, language level, documents in hand — scores the answers, and passes the agency a qualified applicant instead of a one-line enquiry. Runs entirely in Persian, including the right-to-left keyboard flows people actually use.
